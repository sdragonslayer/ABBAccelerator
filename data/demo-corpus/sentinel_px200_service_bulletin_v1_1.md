# Service Bulletin SB-PX200-04

**Synthetic demonstration bulletin — Applicable to Sentinel PX-200 — Version 1.1**

This bulletin was created for the ProofRAG hackathon prototype. It does not describe an ABB product and must not be used to service real equipment.

## Subject

Reduced cooling-filter inspection interval in dusty or fiber-rich environments.

## Applicability

This bulletin applies to synthetic PX-200 controllers installed in woodworking, textile, aggregate-handling, or other locations where airborne dust or fibers can accumulate at the enclosure inlet.

## Updated requirement

For affected installations, inspect the cooling filter every **250 operating hours** or monthly, whichever occurs first. This requirement supersedes the 500-hour normal-condition interval in manual version 1.0 for the environments listed above.

Record filter condition and heat-sink temperature in the maintenance log. If two consecutive inspections find a blocked filter, assess enclosure placement and consider a higher-capacity approved filtration kit.

## E-17 clarification

An E-17 event does not by itself prove that the cooling fan has failed. A blocked filter, obstructed exhaust, loose J4 connector, or high ambient temperature can produce the same event. Follow the complete E-17 procedure and diagnostic fan test before replacing the fan.

All electrical inspection remains subject to the lockout/tagout and absence-of-voltage requirements in the base manual.
